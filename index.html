A bit about me

I like building the whole thing.

Good AI products need more than a model. They need a clear purpose, a well-built application, and a smooth experience all the way through.

+

I'm a software engineer focused on AI, with a hands-on, end-to-end approach to building applications. I enjoy connecting the pieces: understanding the problem, shaping the product, developing the interface and backend, integrating AI capabilities, and bringing everything together for launch.

My goal is straightforward: build software that feels clear and dependable, with AI doing meaningful work for the people using it.
